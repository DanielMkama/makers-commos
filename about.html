About

Building the infrastructure around the people building what’s next

Makers Commons is a growing ecosystem that connects builders with the people, programs, partners, and opportunities that help them go further.

Our mission

We believe the people building what’s next move faster when the groundwork is already there: introductions, funding paths, workspace, and mentorship. Makers Commons exists to be that groundwork, a shared layer of support that any builder can plug into.

What we do

We run programs that take an idea from first sketch to first customers, operate the Makers Cafe as a place to work and meet, and maintain a partner network that opens doors to capital, distribution, and talent.

Who it’s for

Founders, independent makers, and small teams at any stage who want a community around the work instead of building in isolation.
